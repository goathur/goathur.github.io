Digital Metronome

Skills Used

Computer Aided Design
Rapid Prototyping
Microcontroller Programming
Breadboarding

Programs Used

SolidWorks
Python
OrcaSlicer
As a hobby, I am currently learning to play the piano and what I'd usually do to practice being on rhythm is searching for an online metronome. However, this isn't efficient, and I thought it would be great to make my own metronome from scratch, so I wouldn't have to look it up every time on my phone (and also reduce my screentime).
Originally, I wanted to base my metronome off this. However, I also wanted to know what exact BPM the metronome was playing at without having to do any sort of guesswork, similar to the online ones I was using. Therefore, I opted to program it.
Utlizing Thonny to code with Python, I developed a microcontroller program to control a rhythmic beep through the use of mechanical switches to increase or decrease the speed at which the beeps are going at based on its BPM. The speed at which the beep is going at is also coded to be displayed as a value on an LCD screen.
Not only did I develop the software for my metronome, but also the hardware. I designed the case and the mounting tray for my electronic components through the use of SolidWorks to create a CAD model of it, and then went through a series of iterations on my case design through rapid prototyping on an FDM printer.
Here is a video of me demoing my metronome. The piece that I'm playing is "Song of the Dark Woods" by Elie Siegmeister.
